## Brand & Style

The visual narrative balances high-end eco-hospitality with rich cultural heritage. Inspired by the architectural philosophy of Geoffrey Bawa, tropical modernism, and Sri Lanka's geography—from high-altitude Ceylon tea estates to terracotta rock fortresses and the Indian Ocean coastline—this design system evokes serene luxury, authentic craftsmanship, and bespoke exploration.

### Visual Aesthetic & Movement
- **Modern Tropical Luxury:** Spacious composition with deliberate airiness, balancing refined typography with tactile earth tones and lush botanical depths.
- **Editorial Organicism:** Layouts mimic premier travel monographs through high-contrast typography, large-format imagery, delicate divider borders, and warm linen canvas surfaces.
- **Tone & Mood:** Grounded, evocative, prestigious, and effortless. It avoids both sterile modern SaaS austerity and kitschy tropical clichés, cultivating an ambiance of serene discovery and cultural depth.

## Colors

The palette reflects Sri Lanka's contrasting natural ecosystems and heritage pigments.

### Roles & Semantic Application
- **Primary (`#164E3D` / Deep Ceylon Forest):** Anchors high-priority actions, primary buttons, major navigation headers, key section backgrounds, and prominent branding moments. Evokes high-country tea plantations and ancient jungle canopies.
- **Secondary (`#D97706` / Sigiriya Ochre & Sunlit Amber):** Reserved for accent items, verified credentials, pricing highlights, active states, and ratings. Captures the warmth of baked terracotta and afternoon sun on ancient brickwork.
- **Tertiary (`#0D9488` / Coastal Ocean Cyan):** Used for coastal experiences, ocean expeditions, interactive tags, and refreshing status indicators.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FBF9F4` / Warm Ivory Canvas & `#F4EFE6` / Sand Surface):** Replaces cold stark white for canvas backgrounds. Delivers warm, eye-comforting editorial reading.
- **Neutral Ink (`#1A231F` / Deep Charcoal Slate):** Provides high-contrast, WCAG AAA compliant text legibility with a soft botanical undertone, avoiding pure digital black (`#000000`).
- **Surface Elevation Containers:**
  - `surface-base`: `#FBF9F4`
  - `surface-raised`: `#FFFFFF`
  - `surface-sunken`: `#F4EFE6`
  - `surface-inverse`: `#0F382A`

## Typography

The type system pairs the editorial elegance of Playfair Display with the humanist geometry of Plus Jakarta Sans.

- **Editorial Serifs (Playfair Display):** Conveys bespoke heritage and high-end hospitality. Reserved for hero headers, promotional titles, quote highlights, and card headline locks.
- **Modern Grotesque (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Delivers clean readability for detailed tour itineraries, complex pricing tables, booking filters, badges, and long-form narrative guides.
- **Letter Spacing & Casing:** Uppercase transformations are strictly reserved for `label-sm` category headers, currency indicators, and verified badges to impart structured authority without shouting.

## Layout & Spacing

A 12-column fluid grid system governs wide screens, transitioning to 8 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile.

- **Desktop (min-width: 1200px):** 12 columns, 1.5rem gutters, max-width container set to `1280px` with 3rem outer margins.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8 columns, 1.25rem gutters, 2rem outer margins.
- **Mobile (below 768px):** 4 columns, 1rem gutters, 1.25rem outer margins.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Generous vertical section spacing (4rem to 6rem) mimics luxury travel broadsheets, allowing scenic imagery and curated packages room to breathe.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses ambient warmth rather than synthetic greyscale dropshadows.

- **Tonal Layers:** The base application sits on Sand Canvas (`#FBF9F4`). Interactive layers (cards, sheets, modals) step up to Crisp White (`#FFFFFF`) or sink to Linen Muted (`#F4EFE6`).
- **Warm Ambient Shadows:** Shadows utilize deep forest and terracotta color-tinting (`rgba(22, 78, 61, 0.06)` combined with `rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.04)`) with generous blur radii and zero harsh directional offsets, producing a soft natural glow reminiscent of tropical dusk.
- **Hairline Framing:** Cards and elevated containers utilize a delicate 1px low-contrast boundary (`rgba(22, 78, 61, 0.08)`) to preserve crisp contours against light backdrops without aggressive drop shadows.

## Shapes

The geometry uses balanced curvature (`roundedness: 2`), reflecting the architectural curves of verandahs, ocean lagoons, and organic landscape silhouettes.

- **Base Corner Radius (0.5rem / 8px):** Form fields, list items, mini chips, and tooltips.
- **Large Corner Radius (1rem / 16px):** Itinerary cards, destination showcases, hero media frames, and slide-in panels.
- **Full Pill (`9999px`):** Status badges, verified agency marks, filters, search query pills, and primary CTAs.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive CTAs
- **Primary CTA:** Deep Ceylon Forest background (`#164E3D`), pure white text, pill-shaped (`9999px`), with generous horizontal padding (`space-lg`). Hover state scales up warmth with a subtle shift to `#0F382A` and a glowing ochre halo.
- **Secondary CTA:** Warm Ochre outline (`#D97706`), transparent background, Deep Charcoal text. On hover, fills with `#FEF3C7` (light amber wash).
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Forest Green text with an animated 1.5px underline that expands from center-out on hover.

### Badges & Chips
- **Verified Agency Badge:** Pill shape, pale forest background (`#E6F4EA`), Forest Emerald border and icon (`#164E3D`), paired with small uppercase typography (`label-sm`).
- **Rating Chip:** Soft Amber fill (`#FEF3C7`), Sigiriya Amber text (`#B45309`), containing a solid golden star icon (`#F59E0B`).
- **Category Filter Chips:** Neutral sand background (`#F4EFE6`), borderless in resting state. When selected, transitions to solid Ceylon Forest (`#164E3D`) with white text.

### Cards & Itinerary Listings
- **Destination & Tour Cards:** Set on pure white (`#FFFFFF`) with 16px corner radius (`rounded-lg`), hairline border (`rgba(22, 78, 61, 0.08)`), and warm ambient lift on hover (`translateY(-4px)`).
- **Card Media:** Aspect ratio `4:3` or `16:9` with subtle zoom on card hover. Pinned to the top are floating pill badges: regional location (left) and dynamic pricing/duration tag (right).
- **Body Content:** Playfair Display headline followed by bulleted travel amenities (private driver, boutique villa, UNESCO site pass) using botanical green checkmarks.

### Inputs & Search Bars
- **Concierge Search Bar:** Floating pill-container with combined multi-parameter fields (Destination, Dates, Travel Style) separated by light vertical dividers (`#E5E7EB`).
- **Input Fields:** Generous interior height (48px), light sand background (`#F4EFE6`), resting 1px border (`#E2DDD4`), transitioning to Deep Ceylon Forest on focus with a 2px ochre ring.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Circular and rounded-square selectors with Forest Green checks, framed in 1.5px warm slate boundaries. Checked states fill with solid `#164E3D`.
